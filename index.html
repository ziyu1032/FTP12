Alex: Good morning, everybody! Alex here.
Shamei Village — a village that has become a new shiny model of village development.
The first thing I want to say is its unique location. We call it "Shan Shui Lin Tian Hu" — mountains, water, forest, fields and lake. There's a mountain, Jinniu Ling, over there. This is pretty unique.
Shamei Village stays right next to the Boao Forum for Asia, so during the season many foreign guests come here. They take a stroll along this beautiful boardwalk.
This whole project of modification was started back in 2018. Since then, so many things have changed — and it's just stunning. Every time I come here I find something new.
A must-try: Qingbuliang (a sweet coconut dessert soup)! And this part is called "Shamei Impression" — with beautiful architecture, many homestays and restaurants. Basically it has everything you need when you're traveling on vacation.
